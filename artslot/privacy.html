Privacy Policy

ArtSlot: Commission Manager · Last updated: 26 November 2026
In short: ArtSlot has no accounts and no servers. Everything you enter — clients, commissions, payments, pictures — stays on your phone. We never receive it. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob.

Who we are

ArtSlot is published by Monolith Studio Apps, a brand of Stigma Studios di Truisi Antonino (sole proprietorship), Via Sant'Ubaldo 10, 15121 Alessandria, Italy, VAT IT02701100063. Contact: antonino.truisi@stigmastudios.com.

Data you enter in the app

Your profile, clients and their contacts, commissions, prices, payments, expenses, notes, time entries and pictures are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party. You decide what to export or share (PDF, images, CSV, backup files) and with whom, using your phone's share menu. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything; backups you exported stay where you saved them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a small banner and, occasionally, a full-screen ad. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and interaction data to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time in Studio › Ad privacy options. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

ArtSlot Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the app only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase. ArtSlot Pro removes all ads.

Notifications

Deadline reminders are created on your phone and shown by your phone. No data leaves the device for this.

Permissions

Internet (ads and purchases only), notifications (deadline reminders), billing (in-app purchase). Pictures are chosen by you with the system picker; the app does not access your gallery on its own.

Children

ArtSlot is a work tool for artists and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Since we do not hold your app data, you control it directly on your device. For any question about data processed by Google for ads, see Google's privacy policy. For anything else, write to us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Dati che inserisci nell'app

Profilo, clienti e relativi contatti, commissioni, prezzi, pagamenti, spese, note, tempi e immagini sono salvati solo nella memoria privata dell'app sul tuo dispositivo. Pubblicità (Google AdMob)

La versione gratuita mostra un piccolo banner e, di rado, un annuncio a schermo intero. Gli annunci sono forniti da Google AdMob, che può raccogliere identificativi del dispositivo (come l'ID pubblicità), indirizzo IP, posizione approssimativa ricavata da esso e dati di interazione per mostrare, misurare e limitare gli annunci e prevenire frodi. Nello Spazio economico europeo, nel Regno Unito e in Svizzera ti viene chiesto il consenso tramite il messaggio di Google, e puoi cambiare scelta in qualsiasi momento da Studio › Privacy della pubblicità. Approfondisci: Come Google utilizza le informazioni dei siti o delle app che usano i suoi servizi. Puoi reimpostare o eliminare l'ID pubblicità dalle impostazioni di Android.

Acquisti

ArtSlot Pro è un acquisto unico gestito da Google Play. Non riceviamo i tuoi dati di pagamento; l'app verifica solo con Google Play se possiedi l'acquisto. ArtSlot Pro rimuove tutta la pubblicità.

Minori

ArtSlot è uno strumento di lavoro per artisti e non è destinata a minori di 13 anni.
